How it works

Two photos make a bag.

  1. 01

    Find it

    Spot loose litter anywhere — your lane, the park, the road outside. Snap it where it lies.

  2. 02

    Drop it

    Carry it to your society's registered drop point and snap it going in.

  3. 03

    Earn it

    The pair is checked — place, time, no repeats — and 30 coins are minted to you.

Two ways to help

Pick it up — or point it out.

Collect

Loose litter, in your hands.

Wrappers, bottles, cups — surface litter only. Two photos, a drop at your society's point, and coins land in your balance.

  • Earns 30 coins a bag
  • Mystery 2× bonus, sometimes
  • Builds your streak

Report

Too big to carry? Point it out.

A dumping pile or a blocked drain gets a photo, not your hands. Each report becomes a case your RWA, then the municipality, can act on.

Hands off. Never reach into drains or sludge — just photograph it.
