Two ways to help01 · Collect

Pick it up —

Loose, light, in reach: wrappers, bottles, cups. Pick it up by hand, take two photos, drop it at your society's point, and coins land in your balance.

  • 30 coins a bag
  • Mystery 2×, sometimes
  • Builds your streak

Two ways to help02 · Report

or point it out.

Too big to carry? A dumping pile or a blocked drain gets a photo, not your hands. Each report becomes a case your RWA, then the municipality, can act on.

Hands off. Never reach into drains or sludge — just photograph it.
